# UC-053 to UC-058: The periodic report and the report files

**Purpose:** issue the numbered periodic report that replaces the manual statement, and give it and the other reports as files: the periodic report as a PDF laid out as report 23 and as a workbook with live totals; the VAT statement as the accountant's workbook; an item's report and the contracts report as a PDF and a workbook.

**Who uses it:** whoever holds `SeeReports` («يرى التقارير») reads a report, seeing only their own partner line (BR-31). Whoever holds `GenerateReports` («يُصدر التقارير») issues the next one and takes every report's Excel and PDF; a file's رأس المال holds only the partners its reader may see (`PartnerSight`), its اجمالي الكل still the whole capital, as the summary's line is (BR-31). `MoveMoney` no longer issues, and the files no longer need `SeeAllCapital` (redix-api #131).

| Route | Needs | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /projects/{id}/periodic-reports` | `SeeReports` | The reports issued, newest first |
| `GET /projects/{id}/periodic-reports/next` | `SeeReports` | The next number, and what waits for approval |
| `POST /projects/{id}/periodic-reports` | `GenerateReports` | Issues the next report as a snapshot that never changes |

| `GET /projects/{id}/reports/contracts/pdf`, `/xlsx` | `GenerateReports` | The contracts report as files |
| `GET /projects/{id}/reports/contracts/{contractId}/pdf`, `/xlsx` | `GenerateReports` | One contract's report as files, from the item report's writers, for its ⋮ on Contracts (the owner, 12 Nov 2026) |

**One table design (UC-067, FR-46).** Every sheet but الخلاصة is a list of tables written by `statement.Book`, the one layout; a builder (`reports.sheetOf`, `reports.contractsWorkbookOf`) only says what each table holds; رأس المال is an item like any other, its sheet a table per partner (redix-api #109). Every table prints its own columns, nothing fixed (redix-api #129, R1, R4): the columns of its item that print in the reports, in their order, «الوصف» first but on رأس المال, where «نوع الحركة» comes before it, each headed by its own name, then its amount column, headed by that column's name; a purchase table is never given what only a payment has, nor a payment what only a purchase has (`ReportColumns`). «سعر الوحدة» and «العدد» appear only on a table whose item has them, and «رقم الفاتورة» on none: a bill number entered before redix-api #96 D2 stays in `Invoices.Number`, for the VAT statement and the lists. A list's cell holds its choice's name; a file's says «فتح المرفق». «الوصف» is the entry's one description. The PDF draws the same tables. A report issued before redix-api #129 kept no columns of its tables, so its files print the amount alone.

**One workbook (redix-api #129).** The periodic workbook's sheets follow the order الخلاصة reads them: the revenue side's categories, then the expense side's, each category's items in order. A hidden sheet, «القوائم», comes last: a block per list a table's cells choose from, titled with its column, its choices under «الاختيار» and their signs under «الإشارة»; the project's suppliers, accounts and payment methods, each own list such as «نوع القيد» and «نوع الحركة», and a contract's two places. Every list cell has an Excel data validation reading its block, an empty cell allowed where entry allows one, the supplier among them. Each summary line's name opens its sheet, a link to a place in the workbook, and each sheet has «← الخلاصة» back, two columns after its tables. Every sheet prints on A4, one page wide and as many tall as it needs, across the page when its tables are eight columns wide or more; what prints is the tables alone, so the link back never does; and at its foot the sheet's name on the left, the page of the pages in the centre, and the project with the report's number on the right (`StatementBook`, `ChoiceLists`, `StatementStyle.Print`). An item's and the contracts' workbooks print the same, the project alone at the foot. A table is a title row holding only its title, a header, its numbered rows, then its lines, each on a row of its own: الاجمالي; المتبقي and مبلغ الاتفاق when an agreed amount was filled in; its percentage when the sheet calculates one. A sheet of more than one table ends with اجمالي الكل, then نسبة الكل with percentages. Every amount sits in the sheet's last column; a narrower table stretches its first column. Every sheet, الخلاصة included, fits each column to its content up to 45 characters and wraps longer text (`StatementStyle.Fit`), so nothing is cut on screen or on paper. The capital sheet always calculates shares (نسبة المشاركة); an item does when it chose to (`Items.ShowsTablePercentages`). Every line of the summary has its own sheets, and no sheet serves two lines: a line is a category, a sheet is an item.

A report keeps every figure as issued, in `PeriodicReports.Snapshot`. What was approved after the previous report is new and shaded green, whatever its date (BR-26). `ReportFormats` is the one owner of how a file writes a number, a date and a sheet name, and of the embedded Arabic font (NFR-13). The PDFs use QuestPDF under its Community licence; the workbooks use ClosedXML.

**Not proven:** AC-29.1, AC-45.2 and AC-47.1's totals are golden tests against report 23 and the accountant's file, which need the history of Project A imported (UC-052). AC-33.1's "opens in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers" has not been tried in those programs; the tests open each file with ClosedXML. Findings row 119 says what is left.

Each test named `Class.Name` is .NET's, replayed against Go as `TestReplayDotNetsAnswers/Class.Name`; one named `Test…` is Go's own.
